    How it works

    One hour, one resurrection.

    Necro runs the same ritual 24 times a day. Here's every step, and the rules that keep it fair.

    One round
    :00
    :50
    :00
    :00

    Snapshot

    A new ballot of ten opens and everyone's $NECRO balance is recorded.

    :00 – :50

    Vote

    Holders vote. More $NECRO means a heavier vote, based on the snapshot.

    :50

    Lock

    Voting closes and the winner is set. No more changes.

    :00 – :05

    Pump

    The hour's fees buy the winner in five small buys to limit slippage.

    What makes a coin revivable

    Thousands of coins die. Only the ones that pass all four checks reach the ballot.

    01

    ETH left in the pool

    The pool needs some ETH left so a buy actually moves the price and can be sold back.

    02

    Dev holds under 2%

    So a pump never turns into the dev's exit.

    03

    Dead a week, sell-tested

    It has to stay dead for 7 days, and a test sale has to go through, so no honeypots.

    04

    One pump a day

    A winner sits out 24 hours so the ballot keeps moving.

    Questions

    Why isn't my coin on the ballot?

    It failed at least one of the four checks. Search it in the Graveyard and you'll see exactly which one.

    How are votes counted?

    Your vote is weighted by how much $NECRO you held when the round opened. Buying after the snapshot doesn't add weight until the next round.

    Can the same coin win every hour?

    No. A winner sits out for 24 hours before it can return to the ballot.

    Does a pump guarantee the coin goes up?

    No. It's a real buy, so it adds buying pressure, but the price after that depends on the market. Small funds mean small moves.

    Can I check where the money went?

    Yes. Every pump is listed in the Pump log with its transaction on the Robinhood Chain explorer. Both wallets are public: the Necro dev wallet that collects the fees, set at launch, and the pump wallet that makes the buys, set at launch.

    Which coins can be on the ballot?

    Coins launched on pons that peaked above $10K, then fell 90% or more and went quiet. pons pools are locked, so their liquidity can't be pulled.
